# Home (/) — surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Scope: src/pages/Home.tsx plus the shared chrome (Nav, MobileMenu, Footer, AnnouncementBar, WhatsApp float), which the user approved redesigning site-wide.

Audience and job: women 20–45 in CDMX/Metepec, about half from Meta ads on a phone and half from Google with buying intent. They need the price, relief from the fear of pain, real proof and the right branch, then they open WhatsApp with context. No form: LeadForm and LEAD_MAGNET leave the home.

Proof on hand: procedure videos, before/after photos, 16 Google reviews, 5 branches, prices from precios.ts (Full Body $10,500 for 8 sessions). The 4.9 rating stays out of the headers until it is verified.

Sequence: hero → prices (Full Body plus Axilas, Bikini, Piernas completas, Rostro) → pain and Protocolo Expert 8® → real results → reviews → other services → branches → short FAQ → closing offer with WhatsApp.

Open: the font pair is chosen in the build (a sober workhorse, not Bodoni/Inter).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard of a premium clinic landing, executed at full craft. It refuses AI atmosphere: gradient text, blobs, particles, glass, sheens, pills everywhere, a fantasy hero.

OWN-WORLD: A clean light ground (warm white) with near-black ink. Dusty pink (blush) is the single brand color, used for featured fields and the primary price card, never as a gradient. Real photography gets one consistent crop and grade. Shapes are rectangular with small radii, hairline dividers and tabular figures for prices. There is one button language: a solid ink button for WhatsApp and a text link for secondary actions.

STORY: The visitor learns what Cire does, what Full Body costs and that it doesn't hurt much (sapphire tip). She sees real results and real reviews, finds her branch and taps "Cotizar [servicio] por WhatsApp".

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a phone, a real procedure image fills the top ~45%. Below it sit a solid H1 "Depilación láser diodo en CDMX y Metepec", one line of support, a full-width WhatsApp button in the thumb zone and a "Ver precios" link. On desktop the photo is on the right half and the copy plus actions on the left, with the price block starting just above the fold.

FORM: canon (category standard), user-chosen; seed: seed:59264c89a6bab3d9. Quality bar: premium aesthetic and hair-removal clinic sites.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
